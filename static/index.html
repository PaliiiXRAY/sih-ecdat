Technical Architecture, Coverage & Limitations

↓
Detects & Analyzes:
  • Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) Parsing: Discovers cryptographic function calls, imports, and algorithm instantiations across standard libraries.
  • CycloneDX CBOM Standard: Generates standardized Cryptographic Bill of Materials in machine-readable JSON format.
  • NIST PQC Standards: Replaces key establishment with ML-KEM (FIPS 203) and digital signatures with ML-DSA (FIPS 204).
  • Prototype Pattern Coverage: ~85% of tested standard-library cryptographic patterns.
Intellectual Honesty & Limitations:
  • Planning Horizon: The 10-year quantum horizon is a configurable planning assumption, not a deterministic prediction.
  • Runtime Secrets: Does not intercept dynamically loaded runtime keys or unparsed compiled binaries (planned for Phase 2).
  • Resilience Claim: Systems are described as "Quantum-ready" or "No known vulnerable primitives detected", acknowledging ongoing cryptographic research.

RSA-2048: Quantum Risk

auth/keys.py:18
Why is this risky?

RSA relies on integer factorization. A sufficiently capable quantum computer will break this in polynomial time using Shor's algorithm.

Business Impact:

Customer authentication and long-lived encrypted financial transactions can be intercepted today and decrypted later by quantum adversaries under "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later".

What should you do?

Migrate key establishment to NIST FIPS 203 standardized ML-KEM-768.
